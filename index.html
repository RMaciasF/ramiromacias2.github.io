About Ramiro

Penetration Tester / Red Team Operator · Toronto, ON (remote-friendly)

I get paid to think like the person your defenses are built to stop. My day job is running internal and external penetration tests, chasing Active Directory attack paths, and testing web applications — REDLOG is where the interesting parts end up once the client report's already been sent.

Outside of client work I run a home lab, keep a running list of tools that actually hold up under pressure, and spend a fair amount of time turning red-team findings into detections — a finding nobody can detect next time isn't worth much.
